# Synthetic scenario dataset (feature level)

> **SYNTHETIC DATA. Not observations.** Every row has `data_source = SYNTHETIC`, `observation_type = SYNTHETIC`, `timestamp_source = SYNTHETIC`, `gps_source = SYNTHETIC`, `location_source = SYNTHETIC_REFERENCE`. Synthetic data does not replace real observations. It stress-tests the existing risk/event logic with controlled feature-level scenarios; it says nothing about how often these situations occur on real roads or whether the engine is correct on real driving.

Generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_scenarios.py` (generator 1.0.0, schema sim-1.0, seed 42) into `data/simulated/` (`scenarios.jsonl`, `scenarios.parquet`, `schema.json`, `manifest.json`). The real event dataset `data/events/` was only read (sha256 of `events.jsonl` recorded in the manifest) and is unchanged.

**How rows are made.**
- The generator constructs image-space detections and per-frame driver signals at a fixed 10 Hz cadence. No video or images exist.
- These inputs are fed through the **existing, unmodified** components: IoUTracker, the driver temporal logic, RiskEngine (default configuration) and EventRecorder.
- Engine fields are therefore computed from synthetic inputs, and are synthetic too.
- The driver logic first receives 6 s of synthetic driver history (not emitted as rows), so rolling-window driver features have a baseline at t = 0.
- Expectations were written before the engine ran and were not adjusted to its output.

## 3. Timestamp range

- **`timestamp`** (seconds within each instance): 0 to 6.9 s, in fixed 0.1 s steps.
- **`synthetic_datetime`** (synthetic clock): 2026-01-15T09:00:00.000+05:30 to 2026-01-15T09:46:04.900+05:30.
- Instances are laid out 120 s apart on the synthetic clock.
- These are not capture times, and no synthetic timestamp appears in a LOCAL_REAL record.

## 4. Synthetic GPS range

- **Latitude:** 28.60411 to 28.62350.
- **Longitude:** 77.19939 to 77.21792.
- **How it's generated:** Synthetic GPS around a generic Indian urban-road reference point (28.6139, 77.209) with +-0.01 deg start perturbation per instance; gps_source=SYNTHETIC, location_source=SYNTHETIC_REFERENCE. Not a collection location. The real dataset keeps GPS null.
- Within an instance, the synthetic trace advances a few metres per second only to lay out plausible consecutive points. It is not a speed claim.

## 9. Mismatches

These are recorded as findings. The risk engine was **not** changed to remove them.

- **`escalating_hazard__v02`**: peak CAUTION not in expected HIGH/CRITICAL. Level counts {'SAFE': 20, 'CAUTION': 30}.
  - At the peak step (t = 4.9 s of 4.9 s): level CAUTION (raw level HIGH), raw score 45.6, hazard APPROACHING_VEHICLE, factors [persistence +10, centrality +10, approach +23.8, image_size +1.8].
  - Engine reason: "Persistent car #1 (observed 4.9 s, 50 detections): image-space box area growing 111%/s, in the central image band."
  - The raw (pre-hysteresis) level exceeded the peak on 1 step(s) (first at t = 4.9 s), but not for enough consecutive steps for the hysteresis to confirm it before the instance ended.

## 10. Limitations

- **Synthetic, feature level:** there are no pixels, and the detector and hand/face models were never run. Detector noise and failure modes are only approximated (confidence jitter, label flicker, missed detections).
- **Designed scenarios:** each is a single road object with smooth scripted motion and a scripted driver. They are not samples of real traffic, and scenario frequencies say nothing about real-world rates.
- **Circular by design:** risk levels come from the same rule engine being tested. Agreement shows the rules behave as documented on these inputs, **not** that they are correct on real roads. There is no ground truth for crashes or near misses.
- **Small reference pool:** parents come from a small real dataset (66 events, short clips, no GPS, no head-away or hands-off events), so the value ranges are narrow and some driver behaviours have no real reference.
- **`trajectory_overlap` is design ground truth:** it is image-space overlap with the central band. It is not a calibrated vehicle path and is not used by the risk engine. It stays null in the real dataset.
- **GPS and time are synthetic reference values** around a generic point. No map, road network or real location is implied, and there is no link between location and risk.
- **Default engine configuration only:** results change if `RISK_*` / `TRACK_*` / driver settings change.
